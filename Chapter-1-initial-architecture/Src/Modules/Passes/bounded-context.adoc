= Task 1

I chose the *Passes* module.

Its role is to automatically create customer passes after contract signing, marking passes as expired (when they are out of date), storing and retrieving information about the passes.

== It is not responsible for:

* contact signing (or creating) process itself, which is the responsibility of the Contracts module: it just registers a pass in response to the contract signing event.

* creating or modifying offers for customers and sending renewal offers when the pass expires

* capturing registrations

== Data that belongs to this module:

There is only one main entity - `Pass` with such attributes (fields):

* *id*

* *customer id* (without customer details!)

* *from date* (date of activaion)

* *to date* (date of expiration)
